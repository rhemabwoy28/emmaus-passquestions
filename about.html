About Emmaus

Emmaus Methodist Pass Questions exists so that a student's preparation depends on effort, not on whether their school has the past papers or their family can afford a bookshop copy.

We organise BECE Mathematics papers by year and topic, keep the marking schemes next to the questions, and make everything work on a phone with a little data. There is no payment and no signup needed to browse.

Honest limits

We are not affiliated with WAEC or GES. Our papers are study copies, not official publications, and we cannot promise a grade.
